# Use-case intake and recipes

Every request is a different use case. Before outlining, classify it, pick the recipe below, and state the choice in one line to the user ("Treating this as an executive briefing: Route B, 8 slides, appendix, Confidential"). Ask only for what you cannot infer, in one grouped message.

## 1. Intake (infer first, ask for the rest)

| Question | Why it changes the build |
|---|---|
| **Who is in the room, and how many?** (one customer, a team, a hall, mixed internal and external) | Decides tone, classification, template (Standard or Large Format), type size |
| **Presented or read?** (speaker with slides, or sent as a pre-read) | Presented: few words, notes carry the script. Read: assertion headlines, visible footnotes, PDF copy |
| **Length and format** (minutes, live, call, hands-on) | Click budget about 3 clicks a minute; hands-on adds exercise and answer slides |
| **Goal** (inform, decide, teach, win a design-in, align) | Sets the arc and the last slide (ask, next steps, recap, discussion) |
| **Classification** | Footer text, what may be said (see `compliance.md`) |
| **Third parties named?** (customer, partner, competitor) | Approvals, logo rules, wording ("work together", not "partnership") |
| **Source material** (folders, old decks, datasheets) | Search first; old and overseas decks are format references only |

If the user gives a one-line brief, build a first pass with `[TO CONFIRM]` marks rather than interrogating them.

## 2. Recipes

Columns: **Route** (C click-through, B deck builder, A template-native), **Look** (the canvas rhythm from `variation.md`), **Size** (slides, notes style).

| Use case | Route | Look | Size and spine | Must include | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Informal conversation or intro** (university, early customer) | C | Dark throughout, one hero slide | 5 to 8 slides, 10 to 20 min, about 30 clicks. Welcome, why we are here, 2 to 4 topics, discussion questions, light next step | Discussion slide with questions; `CLICKS` cues in notes | Never imply funding, equipment, vacancies or approval |
| **Customer pitch or design-in** | B | Dark with a light evidence slide for the proof | 8 to 12 slides plus appendix. Problem, their criteria, reveal, proof with conditions, dated ask (`story.md`) | Conditions in notes on every number; dated ask | Specs only from datasheets; superlatives only with data |
| **Executive or partner briefing** (for example the Alif and ADI briefing) | B or A on Slide Library patterns | Dark opening and close, light dashboard and product slides | 6 to 10 slides plus appendix. Strategic case, "ADI's response", differentiators, example solutions with proof points, appendix | One "customer outcome" takeaway; a proof point per solution | Co-branded: "work together", not "partnership"; partner logo never larger than ADI's; only recommended-for-new-designs parts |
| **Technical deep dive or architecture review** | B | Dark chapter openers, light diagram and table slides | 10 to 20 slides; one block per chapter; appendix for full plots | Block diagram that builds with Morph; known limits | 14 pt floor still applies; diagrams must stay legible |
| **Training or workshop** (for example DataX, Ensemble PM, BLE) | A, or B with `--static` copy | Light-dominant with dark section openers and answer reveals | 20 to 60 slides in modules. Module opener, concept, example, hands-on, "what the scripts did", quiz and answer pairs, recap | Module timings in notes ("Module 2, 15 min"); exercise slide then answer slide as a Morph pair; quick reference in appendix | Code and commands go in a monospace-free way: keep text 14 pt, show in screenshots or short lines; never leave old-template footers (see `new-slides-index.md`) |
| **Product introduction or launch** | B | Dark with product photography | 6 to 10 slides. Problem, product reveal as hero, three benefits, one proof, availability | Availability and ordering must be public and current | New or unpublished products only inside the approved silent-period rules |
| **Live demo** | B | Dark, minimal | 5 to 7 slides, a "Live" section cue | Recorded fallback one click away | Never debug live |
| **Account review or QBR** | B | Dark summary, light data | 5 to 8 slides, assertion headlines | Decision, owner, date | Internal figures only on Internal footers |
| **Internal update or proposal** | C or A | Dark | 5 to 7 slides, answer first | The ask on slide 1 | Internal Only footer; no forward-looking promises |
| **Corporate overview** | A on Standard layouts | Light-dominant, Primary Blue for key messages | 15 to 30 slides | Forward-looking statements slide when financials appear; classification label | Investor numbers only from approved sources; "Our Ambition", not "Vision 2030 / ADI 3.0", externally |
| **Keynote, stage or large venue** | A or B on the **Large Format Venues** template | Mix of dark and light by room lighting | 10 slides, 20 min maximum | Larger type (22, 20, 18 pt); fewer words | Standard template is not for auditoriums |
| **Leave-behind or pre-read** | B `--static` | Light, with the dark look only on the cover and key messages | Same story, assertion headlines, visible footnotes | PDF export; conditions in footnotes | It is a different artefact from the presented deck; say so |
| **Convert an old deck** | A | Follows the old deck's content, new look | Same slide count | Reset slides, replace fonts with Barlow, rebuild footers, fix tables and charts | Old size (for example 11.02 x 6.20 in), old 2021 footers, off-palette colours, Master-named layouts |
| **Org chart or people slide** | A with the Org Chart layouts | Light | 1 to 3 slides | Internal Only footer | Names and roles from the user, never guessed |
| **Aerospace and defense (ADEF) content** | B or A | Dark, restrained | As the audience requires | Corporate Communications review for anything external | No warfare, destruction or violence imagery or wording; extra Legal and Brand approval (`compliance.md`) |
| **Material for media or press** | Not a deck | PDF only | PR sends PDF, not PowerPoint | Key messages: three takeaways | Involve PR before anything goes out |

## 3. Combining recipes

Real requests mix these (an executive briefing that doubles as a pre-read; a training deck with a customer-facing section). Take the spine from the primary use case and the classification from the most restrictive audience, then state both. If a recipe's rules conflict, the stricter one wins.

## 4. Delivery report (every time)

Say in a few lines: the use case you chose, route, slide and click count, the look rhythm and motifs used (`variation.md`), classification, open `[TO CONFIRM]` items, sources used, whether it was rendered, and any brand-rule judgement calls (for example spelling).
