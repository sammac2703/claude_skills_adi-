# Blocks, icons, branding and data helpers (moved from SKILL.md)

### Use ADI's own blocks and icons (all routes)
Prefer the Slide Library layouts and Brand Icon Library icons over hand-drawn cards: add slides on the library layouts (`references/library-layouts.md`), fill placeholders, put ADI icons (`scripts/adi_blocks.py`, icons from `python scripts/extract_icons.py`) in the visual slots, and keep Morph on every slide so the building blocks move with the same transition. Avoid repeating the same card or box on consecutive slides: vary the layout (statement, priorities rows, agenda, timeline, KPI tiles, team success, product features, two-panel, four columns) and use custom icon-and-rule compositions in between. Include the ADI messaging pillars (Intelligent Edge, Customer Success, People and Planet) where the content allows, in slide text or notes (`references/compliance.md` for the approved wording). Branding and motion lessons from the internship deck: call `b.brand(slide, dark)` on every library-layout slide (the layouts carry no logo or slide number; logo top right, white on dark and ADI blue on light); icons white on dark backgrounds and ADI blue on light (check each layout's background first: KPIs plus Side Content is dark); use `b.open_up()` and hairlines instead of stacking boxes; keep builds to 2 or 3 clicks on sparse slides; when neighbouring slides use very different layouts, Morph makes titles fly and looks abrupt, so use a 0.7 s `fade` (`d.transition(s, "fade", 700)`) and keep Morph for slide pairs that share objects. A light variant of a dark-led deck is built by overriding the slide background and recolouring text (see `references/library-layouts.md`). Data slides: `scripts/adi_data.py` has `paginate_table` (long tables split over slides, header row repeated, '(2 of 3)' in the title, rows never split, 14 pt) and `add_chart` (native column, stacked, bar, line, pie, doughnut and area charts in ADI colours with 14 pt labels, light or dark; no combo charts). Both are ideas taken from PptxGenJS, rebuilt on python-pptx.
